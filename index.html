About the Event

Ideas that become
impact.

E-Summit 2026 is an entrepreneurship-focused event organized by E-Cell MET. It provides students with an opportunity to explore entrepreneurship, discover new ideas and interact with people who are passionate about innovation and business.

The summit aims to encourage creative thinking, problem-solving and collaboration through engaging sessions, activities and opportunities to learn from the entrepreneurial ecosystem.

Our Vision

To encourage students to think beyond conventional paths and develop the confidence to explore entrepreneurial opportunities.

Our Mission

To create a platform where students can learn, share ideas, connect with peers and take their first steps toward entrepreneurship.

Who Can Participate?

Students interested in entrepreneurship, startups, innovation, business ideas and personal development are welcome.

What to Expect?

Opportunities to learn about entrepreneurship, interact with fellow students and explore ideas through event activities and discussions.
